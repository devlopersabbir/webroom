## 📝 Full Description (AMO & Chrome Web Store)

```markdown
WebRoom turns every webpage on the internet into a virtual room.

When you and other people visit the exact same URL, WebRoom connects your browsers into a decentralized presence room and displays a minimal live counter in the corner of the page.

✨ KEY FEATURES:

👥 Instant Real-Time Presence: See the live count of active peers browsing the same webpage.
⚡ Zero Central Backend: Operates peer-to-peer with no centralized database, no server tracking, and no external accounts.
🔒 100% Private & Ephemeral: No accounts, no cookies, no persistent IDs, and no logging. When you leave the webpage, your presence is gone immediately.
🎯 Smart URL Canonicalization: Automatically cleans tracking parameters (like UTM tags and click IDs) so everyone on the same article, video, or documentation page joins the exact same room.
🎨 Minimal & Non-Intrusive: Designed with a sleek, dark glassmorphism pill that rests quietly in the bottom-right corner without obstructing website content.

---

🔒 PRIVACY & SECURITY:

• WebRoom does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.
• There are no analytics, ads, tracking scripts, or telemetry.
• Fully open source on GitHub: https://github.com/devlopersabbir/webroom
```

## 🛡️ Privacy Policy Statement (For AMO & CWS Review Forms)

```text
WebRoom operates on a strict zero-data-collection policy:
1. No Personal Data: The extension does not collect, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII).
2. Ephemeral Presence: Peer identifiers are temporary random UUIDs created in memory for the duration of the browser tab session and are never persisted to disk or cloud storage.
3. No Central Server: Presence discovery and heartbeats operate through local peer communication channels without intermediary tracking servers.
4. No Telemetry or Analytics: WebRoom contains no third-party trackers, advertisements, or telemetry endpoints.
```
